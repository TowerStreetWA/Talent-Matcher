FEATURE SPEC:

1. Data model (new tables, don't alter existing ones):
   - spec_lists: id, candidate_id (FK to existing candidates table), created_by (recruiter/user id), title (optional label, e.g. "July shortlist"), created_at, updated_at.

   - A "Generate candidate-ready summary" view or export — a clean, formatted list (job title, company, note) suitable for copying into an email or exporting, so the recruiter can send it to the candidate.
   - Simple status badges/colors so a recruiter can see at a glance which items are approved ("job on") vs still pending.
