# London Insurance Market: Full Directory with Careers Pages

## Overview

The London insurance market comprises five distinct participant types: **Lloyd's Syndicates** (underwritten through managing agents at Lloyd's), **Company Market Insurers** (IUA members writing business outside Lloyd's), **MGAs** (managing general agents with delegated underwriting authority), **Coverholders** (companies authorised to bind business on behalf of Lloyd's syndicates), and **Insurance Brokers** (accredited intermediaries placing risks with markets). As at 31 December 2025, there were 103 active syndicates at Lloyd's, and IUA members collectively earned over £49 billion in premium income in 2024. The MGAA represents over 240 MGA members in the UK and ROI.[^1][^2][^3]

> **Note on Coverholders:** Lloyd's defines a Coverholder (also referred to as an MGA) as a company authorised by a Lloyd's Managing Agent to enter into insurance contracts on behalf of a syndicate. Thousands of coverholders are listed on the Lloyd's ATLAS system. The most prominent UK-based MGAs with coverholder status are captured in the MGA/Coverholder section below. For the full database, use the Lloyd's ATLAS directory at [ldc.lloyds.com/market-directory](https://ldc.lloyds.com/market-directory).[^4]
